Protecting Your Energy
Party Packs + brands is a heavy load
😮‍💨
Rest on arrival is non-negotiable. 30–45 mins. No phone, no DMs. Your brain needs to reset before you can give your brands anything useful.
🏋🏽‍♀️
Gym days (Mon/Wed/Fri) are high-output days. Your body is primed after exercise. Use the morning invoice window hard on these days.
💆🏽‍♀️
Self-care days (Tue/Thu/Sat) are slower days. Use these evenings for thinking work — content ideas, planning, reviewing goals — not execution.
🔴
Signals you're burning out: snapping at customers, skipping devotion, not replying to orders. When you see two of these in one week — slow down before you stop entirely.
