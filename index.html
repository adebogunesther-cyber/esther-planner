Using the Brain Dump
From scattered thoughts to assigned days
1
Dump everything — any task, idea, or to-do that's floating in your head. Don't filter, just empty your mind.
2
Every Sunday during reset — go through the dump, assign each task to a day using the 📅 button. It lands in that day's to-do list.
3
Daily to-do list — at the bottom of each day in Today, you'll see your assigned tasks. Check them off as you go.
4
Clean the dump weekly — mark completed tasks as done. Delete what's no longer relevant. Keep it lean.
